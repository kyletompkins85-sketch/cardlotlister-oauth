All-in price distributions (top pairwise names)

Each chart is the distribution of price + shipping for that player’s listings in this pilot. Pairwise rank uses same–card-type Monte Carlo duels, not this overall histogram — so a player can have a high win_rate without the highest mean_all_in (e.g. many wins on mid-tier parallels vs cheaper peers for the same label). Red dashed line = median all-in.

Bin range: $0–800 (40 bins). Mass above the cap is folded into the last bin.
